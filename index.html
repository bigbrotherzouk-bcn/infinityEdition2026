⚠️Important: tourist tax at campsite check-in

This year, for the first time, each participant must pay the mandatory tourist tax directly to the campsite during the campsite check-in.

The amount is €0.99 per person, per night — €3.96 per person for the full four-night stay. Please bring €3.96 in cash; €4 is also perfectly fine.

This is a mandatory local tax collected directly by the campsite and is completely external to Big Brother Zouk.

We’re sorry we weren’t able to communicate this sooner. Bringing the amount ready will be a huge help in making the campsite check-in quicker and smoother for everyone.

Thank you so much for your understanding and cooperation! ❤️

🏡Arriving at the campsite? Two check-ins!

Remember: first the campsite check-in, then the Big Brother Zouk check-in.

Campsite check-in

When you arrive, go to the reception at Camping Bon Repòs to register and collect your bungalow key.

📋 Check the list at the reception entrance before joining the queue.
Your bungalow assignment and number will be posted there. Find your name first, then tell reception which bungalow you are in. This is the quickest way to keep check-in moving for everyone.
🛏️ VERY IMPORTANT: ask for your bed linen and towels at reception during check-in.
If you forget, you’ll have to come back to reception for them later. xD

🔑 Keys are normally available from 12:00.

⏰ Important entry restrictions

  • B08-01 to B08-13: entry from 15:00 on Thursday.
  • Tiny Suite 5: entry from 15:00 on Thursday.

Entry times remain subject to campsite availability and may change if further reservations are received.

If your bungalow is not ready yet, we’ll have a room where you can leave your luggage while you enjoy the campsite facilities or the beach.

⚠️ Arriving after 21:00?
Let us know in advance. Security will have your bungalow key ready, but you’ll need to visit reception the following day with your ID or passport to complete the official registration.

✈️Airports & transport

🚩 How to get to the event

Get to Barcelona city center (Plaça Catalunya or Sants Estació)

🚌 Aerobús (fastest): From T1 or T2 → Plaça Catalunya

🚆 R2 Train: From Terminal T2 → Sants Estació

Take the R1 train line

From Sants Estació or Plaça Catalunya, take train R1 towards Maçanet-Massanes.

Get off at Santa Susanna

🚶 The station is just a 5 min walk to Camping Bon Repòs.

⏱ Total travel time from Barcelona center: around 60 min.

🚌 Alternative: Direct bus from the airport (limited schedules)

Bus 603 → Av. del Mar - Camping Bon Repòs (9 stops from the airport)

  • T1 (Terminal 1): Platforms 10–11–12
  • T2 (Terminal 2): T2C Interurbans / Intercity
  • 🚶 Then 5 min walk to the campsite

If you haven't booked your flights yet, we recommend flying into Girona Airport and departing from Barcelona Airport, as this is usually the easiest and most convenient option.

❓Frequently asked questions

Is there WiFi good enough for working remotely?

Yes! The WiFi connection works well, so you shouldn’t have any problems working remotely. And if you get to work from a Zouk vacation… lucky you! 😄
