🏡Arriving at the campsite? Two check-ins!

Remember: first the campsite check-in, then the Big Brother Zouk check-in.

Campsite check-in

When you arrive, go to the reception at Camping Bon Repòs to register and collect your bungalow key.

🔑 Keys are normally available from 12:00.

If your bungalow is not ready yet, we’ll have a room where you can leave your luggage while you enjoy the campsite facilities or the beach.

⚠️ Arriving after 21:00?
Let us know in advance. Security will have your bungalow key ready, but you’ll need to visit reception the following day with your ID or passport to complete the official registration.

Big Brother Zouk check-in

📍 Main event hall

🕕 From 18:00 on Thursday

If you arrive on another day, you can complete it when you reach the event.

Our Crew will be waiting for you and will help you with everything. ❤️

  • Full board from Friday breakfast until Monday breakfast

  • All parties in Santa Susanna
The Barcelona parties and special bootcamps are not included in the Full Pass.
